# From a name to a seat

| Stage | What it means | Next |
|---|---|---|
| listed | Worth knowing, scored 0 to 5 with a reason | A first message |
| invited | A first message went out | Up to two short notes, then later |
| replied | They answered | An answer the same day, and a short call offered |
| booked | A 15 minute call is on the calendar, set by the booker | The brief |
| talked | They spoke with the host | A deeper 30 minute call, the invitation, mentorship, or a kind not now |
| offered | The host invited them in | Ask if they are in |
| joined | Paid, or confirmed by the owner | Welcome, first session, who else belongs |

## The invitation, in the host's words

"We're building a small group of MSP owners who help each other grow. There are <seats> seats and I'm putting the room together myself. I'd want you in it because <the one thing this person brings>. Have a look and tell me if it feels right."

Price and how it's paid come from owner.md, said once and plainly when they ask or when the host invites. If it isn't the fit: "I'd love to help you get there. I keep a few mentorship places for that."

## What makes a fit

- Runs or owns a managed service, IT service or security service business.
- Brings something the room doesn't have yet: a vertical, a specialty, a stage, a region.
- Gives as much as they take. Would be glad to be asked.
- Not already a client in a way that makes it awkward, and not on never_invite.

A small first meeting is fine. Two people is a meeting; the question for it is "who else belongs here?"
