# What each function means for the mastermind

It is not sold. The owner is recruiting a council: a few people who are each one of a kind, invited because of what they bring. Nobody is pitched, hyped or hurried; people select themselves. Someone who is not a fit is offered the owner's mentorship, if owner.md has one, or thanked.

## dream-100
The hundred people who would change the room: MSP owners other owners follow, and leaders of the places owners gather (chapter presidents, community founders, authors, respected operators with a rare specialty). People the owner named or met come first. The ask is a conversation; the seat comes later, from the owner.

## dream-500
Owners and operators of managed service, IT service and security service businesses who bring a vertical, a specialty, a stage or a region the room lacks. Ten at a time, each with one true first line. The ask: compare notes on a short call.

## partners
People whose customers or audience are MSP owners: vendor and distributor community managers, consultants, accountants and brokers to MSPs, newsletter and channel authors, event organisers, peer-group hosts who run something different. The ask: one introduction, a guest session for their people, or a shared event. What they get is the owner's to decide.

## cold-email
MSP owners from public directories and the owner's bulk lists whom no other agent is working. One question about their shop, no offer. The drafts export as a CSV for the sending tool.

## lead-radar
MSP owners asking in public, in r/msp and the communities in owner.md. Phrases: "peer group", "mastermind", "how do other MSPs", "anyone else running an MSP", "looking for a mentor", "stuck at", "burned out", "first hire", "pricing my MSP", "should I sell my MSP", "lonely running". A kept one is helped first, about what they asked.

## content
What the owner learned running an MSP: the group mentality, the next level, hiring, pricing, security for small firms, using AI in your own shop. One post and one short video on the days it runs. The invitation page on Fridays.

## speaker
MSP and IT-channel podcasts, vendor events, association chapters, small-business security panels. The angle is always a lesson other owners can use on Monday.

## aeo-seo
The questions an MSP owner asks before joining a room: "best MSP peer group", "MSP mastermind for small MSPs", "peer group alternatives", "how to get past 20 employees as an MSP", "MSP owner coaching". One honest answer page each time, the invitation page as the place it points.

## sales-crm
Answers every reply, gets the short call on the booker's calendar, briefs the owner, writes the personal invitation after a good talk, welcomes who joins, and asks "who else belongs here?" after every session. The people list is the CRM.
